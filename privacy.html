StudioMemory™ Pro Privacy Policy

Last Updated: February 29, 2026
Developer: Brad Freedman
Contact: freewolfsoftware@gmail.com

Summary

We designed the StudioMemory Pro app to work without collecting personal information. This privacy policy explains how and why we handle personal information in connection with the StudioMemory Pro app installed on iOS and macOS devices.

No Data Collection Through App

StudioMemory Pro does not collect users’ personal information in the normal course of use. You may use StudioMemory Pro without an account. StudioMemory Pro does not run advertising or analytics.

Paid Licenses

If you purchase a license to use StudioMemory Pro, the transaction is processed by Apple through the App Store. Apple may collect and use personal information to process payments, manage subscriptions, prevent fraud, and comply with legal obligations.

  • We do not receive your payment details (e.g., credit card number).
  • Depending on Apple’s systems, we may receive limited subscription-related signals needed to unlock features (for example, subscription status/entitlement, and purchase/renewal information).
  • Your license subscription is managed in your Apple ID settings.
  • For details on Apple’s data practices, review Apple’s privacy information and App Store terms.

Beta Testers

If you are invited to test a beta version of StudioMemory Pro, then you may provide us with suggestions for fixes and improvements to StudioMemory Pro, and you may provide us with crash logs or diagnostic information through Apple's TestFlight (in accordance with your TestFlight settings). We may use the suggestions, information, and data you provide to us to fix and improve StudioMemory Pro. We will solely own all intellectual property we create based on the suggestions, information, and data you provide to us.

Support Communications

If you voluntarily contact us by email for technical support, you may provide us with your relevant personal information—such as your email address, your name, and diagnostic information. We use this information solely to respond to you, diagnose issues, and improve technical support. We do not use this information for advertising or marketing.

Sharing of Information

We do not sell personal information. We do not share personal information with third parties except if required by law, or to protect our rights and users (e.g., fraud/security), or if you explicitly request or consent to sharing.

Data Retention

We keep records of support communications only as long as reasonably necessary to address the support request and for reasonable record-keeping purposes, then we delete the records or anonymize them where feasible. We are located in Vancouver, Canada.

Your Choices and Rights

You can use StudioMemory Pro without providing your personal information. If you contact support, you can request deletion of your support correspondence by emailing freewolfsoftware@gmail.com.

Security

We take reasonable steps to protect any personal information you send us (such as support emails). No method of information transmission or storage is 100% secure.

Children’s Privacy

StudioMemory Pro is not directed to children under 18 years of age, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this Policy

We may change this Privacy Policy from time to time. Our collection, use, disclosure, and retention of personal information will be governed by the version of this Policy in effect at that time. If we make changes, we will update the “Last Updated” date above.
