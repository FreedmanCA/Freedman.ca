Summary

We designed the StudioMemory Pro app to work without collecting personal information. This privacy policy explains how and why we handle personal information in connection with the StudioMemory Pro app installed on iOS and macOS devices.

No Data Collection Through App

StudioMemory Pro does not collect users’ personal information in the normal course of use. You may use StudioMemory Pro without an account. StudioMemory Pro does not run advertising or analytics.

Paid Licenses

Transactions are processed by Apple through the App Store. Apple may collect and use personal information to process payments and prevent fraud.

  • We do not receive your payment details (e.g., credit card number).
  • We may receive limited subscription-related signals to unlock features.
  • Your subscription is managed in your Apple ID settings.

Support Communications

If you contact us via email, you may voluntarily share your email, name, and diagnostic info. We use this only to troubleshoot and improve support. We do not use this for marketing.

Sharing & Retention

Sharing: We do not sell personal information. We only share data if required by law or to protect our users.

Retention: We keep support records only as long as necessary. We are located and store information in Vancouver, Canada.

Your Rights & Security

You can request deletion of your support correspondence by emailing us. While we take reasonable steps to protect your data, no transmission is 100% secure.

Children’s Privacy

StudioMemory Pro is not directed to children under 18, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.
